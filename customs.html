Customs and Stories of Myinkaba Village

Customs and stories are important parts of a community's cultural identity. They reflect people's values, beliefs, experiences, and ways of life.

Traditional knowledge and personal stories can be passed from older generations to younger generations through conversations, family activities, and community experiences.

Through this section, we aim to introduce cultural customs and share local stories connected to Myinkaba Village.
