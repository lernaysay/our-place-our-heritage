## Project Overview

Our Place, Our Heritage is a community awareness website focused on introducing Myinkaba Village in Bagan, Myanmar.

The project aims to share information about the village's culture, traditions, local knowledge, and daily life.

## Project Mission

To help people learn about and appreciate the cultural heritage and community life of Myinkaba Village.

- Daily Life
- Food
- Traditional Clothing and Crafts
- Festivals
- Customs and Stories
