Traditional Clothing and Crafts

Traditional clothing and handmade crafts are important parts of Myanmar's cultural heritage. They show creativity, practical skills, and knowledge that can be passed from one generation to another.

The Bagan area is known for traditional arts and crafts, including lacquerware and other handmade products. These crafts help preserve local artistic skills and cultural identity.

In this section, we explore traditional clothing and handmade crafts, with a focus on their connection to Myinkaba Village and the surrounding Bagan community.
