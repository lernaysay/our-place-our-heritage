4. Content and Image Usage Notes
Interviews are used as primary sources for local knowledge and cultural perspectives.
Official websites and external articles provide supporting background information.
Cultural information is checked against community knowledge and available references whenever possible.
Information about the wider Bagan region is not automatically described as unique to Myinkaba Village.
Photographs are credited to their original creators or providers.
Paid or restricted images must not be published without the appropriate license or permission.
Community photographs are used according to the permission given by the person who shared them.
