4. Content and Image Usage Notes
Community interviews are used as primary sources for local knowledge and cultural perspectives.
External articles and official websites are used as secondary references.
Information about Bagan or Upper Myanmar is not automatically presented as a custom unique to Myinkaba Village.
Cultural details are checked against available community information and reliable references whenever possible.
Photographs are credited to their original creators or providers.
Images with paid or restricted licenses must not be published without the appropriate permission or license.
Community photographs are used according to the permission given by the person who shared them.
The project aims to present local culture respectfully and avoid unsupported claims about the community.
